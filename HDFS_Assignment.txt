Setup HDFS on local: Refer- https://dimensionless.in/know-how-to-install-and-run-hadoop-on-windows-for-beginners/
1. Start HDFS and verify that it’s running.
2. Create a new directory /guvi on HDFS.
3. Create a file, name it big, on your local filesystem and upload it to HDFS under /guvi.
4. View the content of /guvi directory.
5. Determine the size of big on HDFS.
6. Print the first 5 lines to file as output.txt from big on HDFS.
7. Copy big to /big hdfscopy on HDFS.
8. Copy big back to local filesystem and name it big localcopy.
9. Check the entire HDFS filesystem for inconsistencies/problems.
